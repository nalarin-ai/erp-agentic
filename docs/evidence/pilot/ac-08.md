# MVP-AC-08 — payment evidence and correct AR

- Criteria: payments require durable evidence, apply to the correct unit AR (including the R-015 shared Contractor account alias for Heavy Equipment), and chat-only/overpay/duplicate/cross-unit attempts are denied with zero mutation.

## How the tests prove it

Suite: `tests/e2e/pilot/test_ac_08_payments.py` (7 tests; harness wires the real `PaymentWorkflow`, `ReconciliationEngine`/`OperatorQueue`, and `ReceivablesAgingReport` over the fixture adapter).

1. Valid-evidence payment on a Heavy Equipment invoice reduces AR on the shared `ACC-CONTRACTOR` destination alias (R-015 mapping) — verified through the receivables report on the HEQ ledger.
2. Chat-only payment claims (no durable evidence ref) → denied `INVALID_INPUT` ×3 variants, zero provider writes.
3. Overpayment → blocked `OVERPAYMENT` before any provider mutation.
4. Duplicate `evidence_ref` retry → replay, never double-applied (AR outstanding unchanged, single provider write).
5. Cross-unit payment (actor without assignment for the invoice unit) → denied `PERMISSION_DENIED`, zero disclosure.
6. Wrong destination account alias → denied `WRONG_ACCOUNT`.
7. Reversal reopens AR, is fully audited, and double-reversal is blocked.

## Findings (non-blocking)

- F-a (LOW): guard ordering — on a fully-paid invoice a duplicate-evidence retry surfaces as `OVERPAYMENT` block rather than `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`; both paths audited with zero mutation (test accepts either code).
- F-b (INFO): fixture adapter money formatting unscaled pre-payment (`1500000`) vs scaled post-payment (`1500000.00`) — cosmetic fixture artifact, not a production defect.
