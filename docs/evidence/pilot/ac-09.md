# MVP-AC-09 — durable audit/readback/redaction

- Criteria: every state-changing journey leaves a complete, ordered, verifiable audit trail; denied actions are recorded with codes; audit payloads are redacted; audit failure never yields false success.

## How the tests prove it

Suite: `tests/e2e/pilot/test_ac_09_audit.py` (7 tests).

1. Full journey (open → set_lines → preview → post → payment) yields complete, ordered audit events per anchor with monotonic timestamps.
2. The `AuditChain` behind the operator queue verifies (`verify_audit()` true) after a full UNCERTAIN payment round-trip including reconciliation.
3. Denied actions appear in `denied_events` with their denial codes (readback of negative paths).
4. No audit blob carries raw `contact_handle` values or secret material (scanned across journey + queue events).
5. `AuditRecord` redaction contract: sensitive payload keys are redacted at record construction.
6. Audit append failure blocks the mutation and rolls back the claim (MutationExecutor) — no false success.
7. Hash-chain tampering is detected by `AuditChain.verify()`.

## Findings (non-blocking)

- F-a (LOW): workflow-level `audit_events()` surfaces are plain in-memory lists without hash chaining; hash-chained durability exists on the `AuditChain` consumers (OperatorQueue, MutationExecutor). Acceptable for MVP evidence; durable chained audit at workflow level is FND-004 lane backlog (cf. existing finding F-009).
